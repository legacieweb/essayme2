Refund Policy

Our commitment to your satisfaction

Refund Eligibility

We strive to ensure complete satisfaction. Refunds are available in the following situations:

  • Order cancelled before work begins - 100% refund
  • Writer failed to deliver on time - Full refund or extension
  • Plagiarism detected in delivered work - Full refund
  • Paper doesn't meet initial requirements - Free revision or partial refund

Refund Timeline

Refund requests are processed within 5-7 business days. Once approved, the refund will be credited to your original payment method. Please note that depending on your bank, it may take 5-10 additional business days for the refund to appear in your account.

Non-Refundable Cases

Please note that refunds are NOT available in the following cases:

  • After 7 days from delivery (unless quality issues)
  • If you have already approved the paper
  • For orders where requirements were changed mid-process
  • Due to user's own failure to meet their institution's requirements

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, contact our support team at support@essayme.com with your order number and reason for the request. We will review your case and respond within 24 hours.
